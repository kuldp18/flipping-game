ROLE:
You are a senior indie game designer + front-end engineer + pixel artist. Your task is to design and implement a **text-based flipping simulator game** with **pixel-art aesthetics** using **plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript only** (no React, no frameworks, no game engines). The game must feel deep, addictive, and systems-driven despite being text-first.

GAME CONCEPT (CORE LOOP):
The player is a small-time reseller who flips items for profit.
1. Visit different thrift stores / markets
2. Buy underpriced items
3. Negotiate, repair, bundle, or upgrade items
4. Sell them through various channels
5. Reinvest profits into skills, tools, inventory space, and aesthetics
6. Scale from street-level hustler → regional reseller → legendary flipper

The game is **turn-based**, **text-driven**, but visually enhanced with **pixel UI elements**, **icons**, and **retro animations**.

---

CORE GAME SYSTEMS:

1. STORES & LOCATIONS
Each location has:
- Name
- Vibe (affects pricing + item types)
- Restock timer
- Hidden rarity bias
- Negotiation difficulty

Examples:
- Dusty Thrift Mart (cheap junk, hidden gems)
- Old Man’s Garage Sale (low prices, high randomness)
- Urban Vintage Store (higher prices, trend-based demand)
- Night Flea Market (rare items, shady deals)
- Estate Sale (high risk, high reward)

Each store visit consumes:
- Time units
- Energy (player stat)

Items are procedurally generated per visit.

---

2. ITEM SYSTEM (VERY IMPORTANT)
Every item has:
- Name
- Category (electronics, clothes, furniture, collectibles, misc)
- Base value
- Condition (Broken → Mint)
- Demand score
- Rarity tier (Common → Mythic)
- Story flavor text
- Hidden modifiers (trend boost, collector interest)

Examples:
- “Cracked iPod Classic”
- “Vintage Analog Camera”
- “Unbranded Leather Jacket”
- “Weird Ceramic Frog”

Condition directly affects:
- Resale value
- Repair cost
- Buyer interest

---

3. NEGOTIATION & SKILL CHECKS
Buying and selling involve **soft skill systems**:
- Charisma
- Market Knowledge
- Luck
- Intuition

Negotiation happens via text choices:
- Lowball offer
- Fair offer
- Walk away
- Bluff
- Bundle items

Skill checks use weighted RNG + stats.

---

4. SELLING CHANNELS
Different platforms have different rules:
- Street Buyers (fast, low margins)
- Online Listings (slow, high margins)
- Collectors (very picky, huge payouts)
- Bulk Resellers (volume-based)
- Auction Events (high volatility)

Selling may take:
- Time
- Listing fees
- Risk of returns/scams

---

5. INVENTORY SYSTEM
Inventory is **limited by slots**.
Each item occupies:
- Small / Medium / Large space

Players must manage:
- What to keep
- What to dump
- What to repair first

Inventory UI is grid-based but text-described.

---

6. SHOP & UPGRADES
Dedicated shop screen for permanent upgrades:

UPGRADE TYPES:
- Inventory Expansion
- Repair Bench
- Market Scanner (reveals hidden item value)
- Negotiation Boost
- Faster Listings
- Trend Analyzer
- Auto-Repair Tools

COSMETIC CUSTOMIZATION:
- UI color themes
- Pixel avatars
- Shop backgrounds
- Text effects
- Cursor styles

All upgrades affect gameplay systems meaningfully.

---

7. ECONOMY & META SYSTEMS
- Inflation over time
- Seasonal trends
- Random events (market crash, viral trends)
- NPC rivals who compete in the market
- Debt & loans (optional hard mode)
